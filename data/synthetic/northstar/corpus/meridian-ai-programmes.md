# AI programmes for employers

Meridian offers two AI-focused apprenticeships designed with employers.

AI & Automation Practitioner (Level 4): 13 months, ideal for upskilling existing staff in AI tools and automation.

Digital & AI Solutions (Level 6 degree apprenticeship): 30 months, with AI modules embedded throughout the programme.

Employers told us that three-year programmes were too long a commitment for fast-moving technology roles, so we redesigned our degree apprenticeship around a shorter timeline.

We have refreshed every module since 2024 to reflect generative AI.
