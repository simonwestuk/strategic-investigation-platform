# Is degree apprenticeship demand collapsing?

Degree apprenticeship demand is collapsing across every subject area, according to several providers we spoke to.

Digital & Technology Solutions programmes have been hit hardest, with employers losing interest in degree-level digital training.

Providers expect the decline to continue for the foreseeable future.
