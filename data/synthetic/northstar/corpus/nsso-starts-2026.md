# Apprenticeship starts by standard: 2025/26 final release

This release reports apprenticeship starts in England by standard for the academic years 2022/23 to 2025/26.

Total degree-level (Level 6 and 7) apprenticeship starts fell by 21% between 2022/23 and 2025/26, driven almost entirely by Level 7 standards.

Starts on the Senior Leader (Level 7) standard fell from 14,000 in 2022/23 to 5,300 in 2025/26, a fall of 62%. Most of the fall occurred after the Level 7 funding eligibility change was announced in May 2025.

Starts on the Digital & Technology Solutions Professional (Level 6) standard rose from 7,800 in 2022/23 to 8,250 in 2025/26, an increase of 6%.

Starts on the Data Science Professional (Level 6) standard fell from 3,100 to 2,350 over the same period, a fall of 24%.

The Applied AI Engineer (Level 6) standard, first available for delivery in August 2024, recorded 1,100 starts in 2024/25 and 2,600 starts in 2025/26.

Starts on the AI & Automation Practitioner (Level 4) standard grew from 1,200 in 2022/23 to 7,800 in 2025/26.

Starts on the Data Analyst (Level 4) standard fell from 11,000 to 9,100.

Chartered Manager Degree Apprenticeship (Level 6) starts were broadly flat at around 9,900 per year.
