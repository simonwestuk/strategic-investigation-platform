# Employer Skills Outlook 2026

Our annual survey of 1,850 employers found that 61% now rank AI and automation skills among their top three training priorities, up from 23% in 2023.

Among employers that use apprenticeships for digital roles, 47% said they would prefer programmes of two years or less, compared with 29% in 2023.

Employers increasingly report moving apprenticeship spend from general digital programmes to AI-specific programmes.

Large employers were more likely than SMEs to report switching provider in the last two years.

The survey did not ask employers which providers they switched to.

Demand for leadership and management apprenticeships at Level 7 fell sharply following the funding eligibility change.
