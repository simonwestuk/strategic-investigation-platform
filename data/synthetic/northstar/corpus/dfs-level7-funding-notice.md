# Changes to Level 7 apprenticeship funding eligibility

From 1 January 2026, levy funding for Level 7 apprenticeships will be available only for apprentices who are aged under 22 when they start, and for existing apprentices who started before that date.

The change applies to all Level 7 standards, including the Senior Leader standard.

Employers may continue to fund Level 7 programmes from their own budgets.

The policy does not change funding eligibility for Level 6 degree apprenticeships.

The department expects funding released by this change to be redirected towards Level 2 to Level 6 provision and new foundation apprenticeships.

Update, 3 November 2025: guidance for providers on transitional arrangements has been published.
