# Employers turn to AI apprenticeships as digital degree programmes face pressure

Providers that launched AI-specific apprenticeships early are reporting strong growth, according to interviews with eight training providers.

Several providers said employers were replacing places on general digital degree apprenticeships with places on AI-focused programmes.

One provider said shorter programme durations had become a deciding factor in competitive tenders with large employers.

Providers without AI programmes reported losing tenders they had previously won.

Not all providers agreed: two said demand for their general digital programmes was stable.
