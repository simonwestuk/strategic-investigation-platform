# Applied AI Engineer (Level 6)

Status: approved for delivery from 1 August 2024. Typical duration: 24 months.

The Applied AI Engineer designs, builds and deploys AI-enabled systems, including machine learning models and generative AI applications, within organisational governance requirements.

This standard is available as an integrated degree apprenticeship.

Related standards: Digital & Technology Solutions Professional (Level 6), Data Science Professional (Level 6), AI & Automation Practitioner (Level 4).

The board's 2026 review notes that the Applied AI Engineer standard has become the fastest-growing Level 6 digital standard since its launch.
