Northstar Learning Group: Degree apprenticeship performance, Q2 2026
Prepared by: Michael Turner, Director of Sales

1. Summary
Degree apprenticeship starts have fallen from 1,500 in 2022/23 to 960 in 2025/26.
In my view the decline is entirely due to the Level 7 funding changes, and no change to our portfolio is required.

2. Regional performance
The North region sales team was restructured in September 2023. DTS wins in the North fell sharply after the restructure, and the decline in the North coincided with the new team structure.

3. Portfolio
Our DTS programme runs for 36 months. The DTS curriculum was last substantially refreshed in 2019.
We do not currently offer an AI-specific apprenticeship at any level.

4. Pricing
Our pricing has been in line with the funding band maximum throughout the period, with no discounting.
