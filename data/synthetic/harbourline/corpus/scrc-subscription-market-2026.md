# UK coffee subscription market 2026

Total coffee subscription renewals in the UK grew 21% between 2023 and 2025.

Espresso bean subscription renewals grew 7% over the period, from 120,000 to 128,000.

Filter coffee subscription renewals fell 12.5%, from 80,000 to 70,000, as consumers shifted to espresso and capsules.

Capsule subscriptions were the fastest-growing segment, more than doubling from 40,000 to 90,000 renewals.

Office subscriptions were broadly stable.
