# Coffee subscriber survey 2026

Price was the most common reason for cancelling a coffee subscription, cited by 54% of people who cancelled in 2025.

41% of subscribers said the ability to pause deliveries was important when choosing a provider.

Subscribers increasingly prefer flexible plans with no commitment.

The survey did not record which provider cancelling subscribers moved to.
