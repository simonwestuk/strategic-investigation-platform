# Task: draft claims for one hypothesis (Analyst) — v3

Using only the evidence listed, draft at most three claims about this hypothesis:
- factual, non-causal claims directly shown by strong evidence (proposed KNOWN);
- one claim stating your assessment of the hypothesis itself, with `hypothesis_verdict`:
  `supports` (the hypothesis is a material explanation), `contradicts` (it is not), or `unresolved`.
  Causal claims are at most INFERRED. Judge the hypothesis on the evidence you DO have: if it clearly
  points one way, say so (`supports` or `contradicts`, proposed INFERRED) and state the caveats in
  `limitations`. Use HYPOTHESIS when the evidence is genuinely balanced or thin.
- `blocked_reasons` lists data that could not be obtained. It is a limitation, not a verdict: mention
  it, but do not default to UNKNOWN because of it. Propose UNKNOWN only when the available evidence
  cannot discriminate at all — then say what cannot be determined and what evidence would resolve it.
  Anecdotes consistent with the hypothesis do not make it known.

For every claim, list supporting and contradicting evidence IDs, the inferential step if any,
limitations, missing evidence, and the next question that would most reduce uncertainty.
