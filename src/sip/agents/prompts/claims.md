# Task: draft claims for one hypothesis (Analyst) — v2

Using only the evidence listed, draft at most three claims about this hypothesis:
- factual, non-causal claims directly shown by strong evidence (proposed KNOWN);
- one claim stating your assessment of the hypothesis itself, with `hypothesis_verdict`:
  `supports` (the hypothesis is a material explanation), `contradicts` (it is not), or `unresolved`.
  Causal claims are at most INFERRED. If evidence is balanced or weak, propose HYPOTHESIS.
- If evidence needed to resolve the hypothesis is unavailable (see blocked reasons), propose an UNKNOWN
  claim that says what cannot be determined and lists the missing evidence. Anecdotes consistent with
  the hypothesis do not make it known.

For every claim, list supporting and contradicting evidence IDs, the inferential step if any,
limitations, missing evidence, and the next question that would most reduce uncertainty.
