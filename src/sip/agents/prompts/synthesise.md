# Task: generate strategic options (Strategy Synthesiser) — v1

Generate 3 to 6 distinct strategic options that respond to the findings. These are recommendations for
human decision-makers, not decisions. There is rarely one correct answer; make the trade-offs visible.

Each option must cite the finding IDs it responds to, and state expected benefits, risks, uncertainties
(including relevant UNKNOWNs), dependencies, reversibility, consequence level and the additional
evidence that would most change the assessment. Always include one option that commissions research on
the most material unknowns, and one baseline option of not changing course.
