# Task: frame the strategic question (Investigation Lead) — v1

Interpret the question. Do NOT try to answer it. Identify:
- question types (diagnostic, prescriptive, evaluative, exploratory);
- the subject being explained, as a short noun phrase (e.g. "degree-apprenticeship enrolments");
- the direction of change if stated;
- the decision context, scope, what is out of scope;
- what a useful investigation must establish (objectives);
- assumptions being made, ambiguities, constraints;
- whether the question is too ambiguous to investigate without asking the requester
  (needs_clarification) and, if so, the clarification questions.
